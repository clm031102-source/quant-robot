## Objective

Test a compact, previously untested CN ETF hypothesis: whether improving and broadly distributed liquidity predicts future ETF returns after excluding historical liquidity-level, amount-level, amount-acceleration, amount-stability, and price-volume duplicates.

This stage can produce only a statistical research lead. It cannot authorize a portfolio grid, walk-forward run, paper signal, promotion, broker access, account read, order placement, or live trading.

## Historical Audit And Legacy Quarantine

The repository already tested these CN ETF liquidity/capacity structures without durable promotion evidence:

- Amihud liquidity levels over 5, 10, 20, 60, and 120 sessions.
- High-liquidity inversions and single-day liquidity resilience.
- Amount level, amount stability, volume change, amount acceleration, demand pressure, and quiet accumulation.
- Liquidity-gated momentum, relative strength, recovery, and defensive low-volatility structures.
- Trend-volume and OBV/SuperTrend capacity repairs.

Those structures are references, not candidates. Window tuning, threshold relaxation, and parameter rescue are prohibited.

The historical `CN_ETF_liquidity_10_top1_cost5_reb5` packet was generated by an obsolete one-split gate that lacked the current fold, adjusted-IC, positive-IC-rate, date, provider, and data-quality requirements. Rebuilding `configs/promotion_gate_cn_etf_candidate_search.json` under the current gate blocks all 270 legacy cases and yields `paper_ready=0`. Historical documents remain evidence of what happened, but their promotion label is superseded and must not seed a new portfolio run.

## Considered Architectures

### Copy The Existing Skip-Momentum Prescreen

This is fast but would duplicate more than 600 lines of statistical and artifact logic, making future fixes diverge across active research families.

### Extract A Shared Statistical Engine With Thin Family Wrappers

This is selected. Pure cross-sectional IC, Newey-West, FDR, quintile, yearly-stability, turnover, and reference-correlation logic moves into one research module. Skip momentum keeps its existing output contract through a thin wrapper; liquidity adds only its family evidence and capacity calculations.

### Build A General Research Framework

This could absorb loading, eligibility, factor computation, rendering, and decisions for every family, but it is broader than the current need and would increase regression risk.

## Frozen Candidates

Higher values always mean a more favorable ETF rank. Every formula uses only signal-date and earlier observations.

| Factor | Formula at signal date `t` | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `etf_amihud_improvement_5_60` | `log(mean(amihud[t-64:t-5]) / mean(amihud[t-4:t]))`, where daily `amihud=abs(return)/amount` | Recent price impact improved relative to a disjoint prior baseline. |
| `etf_amount_participation_breadth_20_60` | Mean over the last 20 sessions of `amount[d] > median(amount[d-60:d-1])` | Participation is persistent across days rather than represented by a single amount spike. |
| `etf_amount_distribution_quality_20` | `1 - sum(amount[d]^2) / sum(amount[d])^2` over 20 sessions | Turnover is distributed through the window rather than concentrated in a few sessions. |

The Amihud means require complete five- and sixty-session windows and use a strictly prior 60-session baseline. Zero or non-positive amount is missing. The amount-breadth baseline excludes the day being classified. Distribution quality requires 20 positive amount observations. No alternative windows, medians, winsorization levels, signs, combinations, or weights may be introduced after observing results.

## Historical Reference Gate

Candidates are compared by same-date cross-sectional Spearman correlation with these 13 already tested exposures:

- `liquidity_5`, `liquidity_10`, `liquidity_20`, `liquidity_60`
- `liquidity_resilience_60`
- `amount_stability_20`, `amount_stability_60`
- `average_amount_20`, `average_amount_60`
- `volume_change_20`, `volume_change_60`
- `demand_pressure_60`, `quiet_accumulation_60`

A row is a hard historical duplicate when maximum absolute mean daily Spearman correlation is at least 0.85. Missing reference evidence fails closed.

## Data And Point-In-Time Universe

Primary local root: `data/processed/tushare_etf_wide_history_2023_2026`.

The current source contains 2020-01-02 through 2024-06-28 history despite its directory name. The analysis window is fixed to those dates. The 2026 final holdout is sealed and prohibited. Any later walk-forward stage first requires a separate, audited 2024-H2 through 2025 backfill.

Signal-date membership reuses the official, point-in-time ETF lifecycle and trailing-only eligibility policy:

- Official ETF flag and valid list/delist lifecycle.
- At least 252 prior observations.
- Trailing 20-session median amount at least CNY 5 million.
- Trailing 20-session stale-price rate no greater than 5%.
- Positive current adjusted price and amount.
- Absolute current adjusted return no greater than 20%.

The static Round25 endpoint-selected universe is prohibited. Cross-sections require at least 30 eligible ETFs.

## Statistical Gate

- Forward horizons: 5 and 20 sessions.
- Execution lag: 1 session.
- Daily test: same-date Spearman Rank IC.
- Dependence correction: Newey-West mean test with lag `horizon - 1`.
- Multiple testing: Benjamini-Hochberg FDR across all 3 x 2 frozen tests at alpha 0.05.
- Mean Rank IC at least 0.02.
- ICIR at least 0.30.
- Positive daily IC rate at least 55%.
- Positive top-minus-bottom quintile return.
- Quintile monotonicity at least 0.70.
- Average top-quintile turnover no greater than 90%.
- At least three usable calendar years and at least 60% positive years.
- Maximum absolute reference correlation below 0.85.

## Capacity Gate

Capacity uses canonical CNY `amount` and a trailing 20-session mean amount (`ADV20`) available at the signal date. For each factor-horizon row, calculate ADV20 across every valid top-quintile asset-date and record its observation count, median, and tenth percentile.

The frozen implementation assumption is a CNY 1,000,000 portfolio with 10 equal positions, giving CNY 100,000 one-way notional per position. The tenth-percentile top-quintile ADV20 must support that position at no more than 1% participation:

`100000 / top_quintile_adv20_p10 <= 0.01`

Equivalently, the tenth-percentile ADV20 must be at least CNY 10 million. Missing capacity observations fail closed. This gate is a feasibility screen, not a cost model or profitability claim.

## Decision Rules

If at least one factor-horizon row passes every statistical, duplicate, and capacity condition, freeze the smallest non-duplicate lead set and its evidence hash. Do not run a walk-forward or portfolio grid until 2024-H2 through 2025 data is backfilled and audited.

If no row passes:

1. Mark `cn_etf_liquidity_capacity` `stop_lossed` with budget 0.
2. Prohibit retries, window tuning, threshold relaxation, parameter rescue, portfolio grids, and walk-forward runs for this structure.
3. Reallocate its frozen 0.35 share exactly as follows: add 0.05 to `cn_etf_volatility_regime`, add 0.15 to `cn_etf_flow_breadth_aggregation`, and add 0.15 to `cn_etf_fund_structure`.
4. The resulting active allocation is volatility 0.35, flow breadth 0.35, and fund structure 0.30.

The zero-lead reallocation is fixed before results and respects the scheduler's 0.35 per-family cap and three-active-family minimum.
